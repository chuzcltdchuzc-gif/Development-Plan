## 1. What "one-day demand test" means

**"One-day demand test" means one defined measurement or analysis day after controlled recruitment.**
It does not mean that recruitment begins and ends in one day, and no material produced under this
playbook may describe or imply a same-day recruitment-and-measurement design. The "one day" refers to
the single predefined day on which recruited respondents are brought through the structured
interview/intake instrument this playbook defines (§7–§10), following the recruitment window at §2 —
**the same single day Decision §17 item 1's duration schedule names as the "one predefined
demand-measurement day" within its days-8–21 range, not a different or additional day.** Decision §17
item 1 does not create a second measurement day, a rolling measurement period, or a per-wave
measurement day; it schedules this playbook's one day within a wider, multi-wave window, exactly as
§2 below now describes.

## 2. Recruitment window

*Recruitment-window length reconciled with the determined duration parameter (Decision §17 item 1) on
Governance Authority parameter incorporation; the single measurement day itself, and its meaning, are
unchanged from §1 above.* Recruitment runs across the days-1–21 window Decision §17 item 1 schedules,
organised as at least two prospective recruitment waves (Decision §14.2), run separately for the
institutional and retail segments (§3), and shall not be compressed to same-day recruitment-and-
measurement under any circumstance. Every respondent recruited in any wave is brought to the single
measurement day at §1 above for their actual interview/intake session — waves may recruit sequentially
or in parallel within days 1–21, but every wave feeds the same one measurement day; no wave schedules
its own separate measurement day. Where operational necessity would compress the recruitment window
below what two genuine, prospectively-defined waves require, that requires an explicit, recorded
justification and Governance's prior awareness before the test proceeds — this playbook does not
pre-authorize a shorter or single-wave window by silence.

**Recruitment waves within this window.** Every recruitment wave conducted within this window is
governed by the Decision's own operative definition at §14.2 — a prospectively documented, separately
dated outreach batch, fixed before that wave's recruitment begins, never a boundary drawn
retrospectively from data that already exists. The wave schedule itself (how many waves, their
opening/closing dates, and their intended recruitment source per wave) must be settled and documented
as an execution prerequisite (§16 below) before any recruitment begins — it is not decided after
recruitment data has already arrived.

## 3. Segment separation

Institutional and retail respondents shall be recruited through **separate channels**, wherever
practicable, and shall be assigned a distinct segment identifier at the point of recruitment — not
inferred, reclassified, or assigned after the fact. Every subsequent step in this playbook (pricing,
questions, analysis, reporting) is performed once per segment, independently. The main Decision's §7
institutional-subtype recording requirement and §8 retail-recruitment requirements govern this step in
full; this playbook adds no exception to either.

**Registered-surveyor classification within the institutional segment.** Where an institutional
respondent claims registered-surveyor status, that claim is checked exactly as the Decision's §7.1
describes — a participant-supplied SURCON identifier checked against an authoritative public SURCON
source where available, recorded as either authoritatively checked or `UNVERIFIED SURVEYOR STATUS`,
never assumed, never bulk-checked, and never falling back silently to unchecked self-report. This
playbook adds no exception to §7.1 and performs no part of that check itself — it only records, at
intake, which outcome applied to each respondent.

## 4. One price per respondent

Each respondent shall be shown, and asked to respond to, **exactly one price point** for the proposed
offering, appropriate to their segment (§3). No respondent shall be shown multiple price points within
the same interview for the purpose of finding an acceptance threshold (a Van Westendorp-style or
similar multi-price elicitation is not authorized by this playbook) — the single-price design is
required so that "price acceptance" (§9 of this playbook, and draft §9/§14) has one unambiguous meaning
per respondent, not a range requiring further interpretation.

## 5. Current or previous price recording

Every respondent shall be asked, before being shown the proposed price point, what they currently pay
or have previously paid for a comparable service, if any, and what they were quoted for one, if any.
This question is asked **before** the proposed price to avoid anchoring the respondent's answer to it.
A respondent who reports no prior spend or quote shall have that fact recorded explicitly (as
"no prior spend/quote reported"), not left blank or inferred as zero.

## 6. Teach-back requirement

Every respondent shall be asked a teach-back question, after the non-adjudication disclaimer (§14
below) is presented and before any commitment question is asked, confirming in their own words their
understanding that LandVault records and verifies evidence and does not determine legal ownership,
adjudicate disputes, or replace government title systems (restating the main Decision's §3 verbatim).
A respondent whose teach-back answer does not confirm this understanding shall have that recorded as a
**failed comprehension check** for that respondent. A recruitment wave with a materially elevated
failed-comprehension rate contributes to an INDETERMINATE outcome at the governance checkpoint (main
Decision §5.3, and §17 of this playbook), not to a discounted or excluded data point silently dropped
from the sample.

## 7. Deduplication

Respondents shall be deduplicated by **both** respondent identity and parcel/case identity before
analysis. A respondent who is interviewed more than once (across recruitment waves, channels, or
segments) shall be counted once in the final analysis, with any duplicate record retained for audit
purposes but excluded from the reported count. A single parcel or case discussed by more than one
respondent (for example, a firm's several employees describing the same institutional case) shall be
flagged for review — Governance's attention is required where this materially affects the institutional
threshold count (main Decision §14), since a case-level rather than respondent-level count could
otherwise inflate apparent institutional interest.

## 8. Chief Surveyor and intermediary referral tagging

Every respondent record shall capture, at the point of intake, whether the respondent was referred by
a Chief Surveyor, a professional-body officer, or another influential intermediary, and if so, by whom
(by role, not necessarily by name, consistent with the data-minimisation principle underlying §17
item 8's Nigerian data-protection gate and the informed-participation step at §21 below). Referred
responses are analysed as a distinct subgroup within their segment, per the main Decision's §10, and
are never merged into the "directly recruited" subgroup for threshold-calculation purposes without
disclosure of the merge. **Access supplied by a Chief Surveyor or other intermediary is not, by
itself, evidence of independent market demand.**

**Referral-concentration ceiling, restated from the Decision's own controlling text (§10):** no more
than 50% of the qualifying institutional commitments counted toward any §14 threshold band may
originate from a single referral source. Exceeding this ceiling prevents a `STRONG` classification
regardless of the raw qualifying-commitment count (Decision §14.1 item 7, §14.3). No referral source
may select which responses are reported or excluded.

## 9. Honest commitment terminology

Every reported demand figure shall be labelled with exactly one of the following three tiers, and no
figure may be described using language implying a higher tier than the evidence supports:

1. **Survey answer: stated-price acceptance** — the respondent indicated, verbally or via the intake
   instrument, that the shown price point is acceptable to them. This is the weakest tier and shall
   never be reported or referred to as "willingness to pay" without the qualifying phrase "stated" or
   "self-reported" attached.
2. **Costly non-financial commitment** — the respondent took an action carrying real cost to them
   beyond stating a price acceptance: booking a specific follow-up meeting, or submitting an eligible
   document for the proposed workflow to review. This tier is stronger evidence than (1) but is not
   itself a financial commitment.
3. **Payment-backed commitment** — the respondent made an actual payment, or a properly governed,
   explicitly authorized refundable deposit (§13 below governs whether this sub-tier may be used at
   all in a given test run). This is the strongest tier.

A report that aggregates figures across these tiers without labelling each figure's tier is
non-compliant with this playbook and shall not be used to support a PROCEED determination at the
governance checkpoint.

## 10. Deduplicated qualifying-commitment count

For the purposes of main Decision §14's threshold bands, a "qualifying institutional commitment" means
a deduplicated (§7), non-contaminated (main Decision §10; §8 above), institutional-segment (§3 above)
respondent record reaching at least tier 2 ("costly non-financial commitment") per §9 above. Tier-1
survey answers alone do not count toward the threshold bands, though they are recorded and reported
as supporting, weaker evidence.

## 11. Segment-specific analysis

Every analysis step described in this playbook (price-acceptance rate, comprehension/teach-back pass
rate, commitment-tier distribution, deduplicated qualifying-commitment count) is performed once per
segment (institutional, retail), and, within the institutional segment, is additionally broken out by
the institutional subtype categories the main Decision §7 requires to be recorded, including the
registered-surveyor / other-institutional-subtype split §7.1 and §14.1 require. **Segment-separated
results are this playbook's primary output, restated from the Decision's own controlling rule at §9**
(remediation of finding C1: this requirement is stated in the Decision itself, not left to this
playbook alone to enforce). A combined administrative volume count may additionally be reported only
under the narrow, disclosure-carrying exception the main Decision §9 permits, and never in place of
the segment-separated figures.

## 12. INDETERMINATE default

Where recruitment falls short of a usable sample size for a given segment — for the institutional
segment specifically, including any shortfall against the minimum-sample precondition at Decision
§14.0 — where the teach-back failure rate is materially elevated (§6), where referral tagging (§8)
reveals recruitment concentrated overwhelmingly through a small number of intermediaries such that
independence cannot reasonably be assumed, where an ordered-sequence violation triggers the
consequence at Decision §14.8 / §22 below, or where results are internally contradictory (for example,
high stated price-acceptance alongside near-zero tier-2/tier-3 commitment with no plausible
explanation recorded), the test's own contribution to the governance checkpoint shall be recorded as
contributing to an **INDETERMINATE** outcome for that segment, per main Decision §5.3. This is the
correct, honest recording in that circumstance, not a result to be avoided by re-interpreting
borderline data more favourably.

## 13. Refundable reservations — excluded absent separate authorization

**A refundable reservation is not included in the operational test authorized by this playbook.**
Collecting any reservation, deposit, or payment of any kind requires its own, separate, explicit
authorization covering payment handling, refund terms, financial handling, privacy, and cancellation
authority — none of which this playbook or the main Decision grants (main Decision §17, §22). If a
future, separately authorized test wishes to use a refundable-reservation design, that authorization
must be obtained before it is added to any version of this playbook; this version does not include it
and may not be silently extended to include it during execution.

## 14. Disclaimer placement checklist

The non-adjudication statement (main Decision §3) or a Governance-approved equivalent shall be
confirmed present, before the test proceeds, at each of the following points — this is a pre-test
checklist item, not a post-hoc audit step:

- [ ] Recruitment advert
- [ ] Landing or registration page
- [ ] Intake form
- [ ] Interview introduction script
- [ ] Teach-back step (§6 above)
- [ ] Any sample output shown to a respondent
- [ ] Commitment or reservation step, if any is used within this playbook's tier-2 scope (§9)
- [ ] Final research report

**Informed-participation and privacy-notice checkpoint, distinct from the above** (remediation of
finding M6): before any interview or respondent data is collected —

- [ ] Privacy notice and informed-participation step delivered and documented (§21 below; Decision
  §25) — this is a separate checklist item from the non-adjudication statement above, not satisfied
  by it.

A test run missing any checked item does not proceed until the gap is closed — this checklist is a
precondition to running the test, not a retrospective compliance note.

## 15. Managed intake or explicit residual-risk disclosure

Every test run shall use either:

1. a **managed intake process** — a recorded, single point of contact (human or a controlled,
   non-production intake form) through which every respondent interaction passes, allowing consistent
   application of §4 (one price per respondent), §6 (teach-back), and §14 (disclaimer placement); or
2. where a managed intake process is not operationally available for a specific channel, an **explicit
   residual-risk disclosure** recorded before that channel is used, naming which of §4/§6/§14's
   controls cannot be guaranteed through that channel and why.

A test run using option 2 for any channel shall flag that channel's results as carrying elevated
uncertainty in the final report, consistent with §12's INDETERMINATE-default discipline where the
uncertainty is material.

## 16. Operational prerequisites before any test date is scheduled

The following must all be true before any actual test date is scheduled or communicated to any
prospective respondent:

1. GD-015 is ratified by the Governance Authority.
2. §14's disclaimer-placement checklist, including its informed-participation/privacy-notice
   checkpoint, is fully satisfied.
3. §15's managed-intake-or-disclosure requirement is satisfied for every recruitment channel to be
   used.
4. Main Decision §17's duration, effort-ceiling, expenditure-ceiling, and incentive figures — now
   determined at Decision §17 items 1–4 — have the operational mechanisms required to apply them
   (§23's effort log, §24's expense and incentive register) actually in place and ready to record
   against those determined figures before any activity begins (a determined figure with no working
   mechanism to track it against does not satisfy this item).
5. The data-retention, access-control, and deletion controls determined at main Decision §17 items 5–6
   and operationalized at §26 above are in place and documented before any respondent data is
   collected.
6. **The Nigerian data-protection review required by Decision §17 item 8 has occurred**, within its
   determined ₦400,000 budget, and the research protocol, privacy notice, and lawful basis or bases it
   produces are in place before any respondent data is collected (remediation of finding M5 — this item
   is execution-blocking, not merely advisory, regardless of the budget being determined).
   **Commissioning the reviewer is not this item's satisfaction.** Decision §17's preamble authorizes
   commissioning, instructing, and paying the reviewer on ratification alone (item 1 above), precisely
   so that this item can ever be satisfied at all; this item itself remains unsatisfied, and this
   prerequisite remains unmet, until the review the reviewer was commissioned to perform has actually
   been completed and its output actually received.
7. The recruitment-wave schedule required by §2 above and Decision §14.2 is documented and fixed
   before recruitment begins.
8. The standardized interview script and interviewer-independence controls required by §19 below
   (Decision §26) are in place.
9. The Project Lead and the named written alternate required by Decision §17 item 9 are named and
   confirmed, and §25 above's escalation procedure is in place, before any respondent-facing or
   SURCON-facing action occurs.

**No retrospective or same-day test date is authorized by this playbook.** The actual test date shall
be scheduled only after all nine conditions above are satisfied, and not before.

## 17. Reporting requirements

The final Phase 0A report produced from any test run under this playbook shall include, at minimum:

- whether the minimum institutional sample precondition (Decision §14.0) is met — the number of valid
  completed eligible institutional interviews remaining after any §22-below/Decision §14.8 exclusions
  and deduplication, stated explicitly, before any other institutional figure is reported;
- segment-separated (§11) response counts, with institutional subtype breakdown (main Decision §7),
  including the registered-surveyor / unverified-surveyor-status split (Decision §7.1, §14.1);
- **every commitment figure reported together with its denominator** (remediation of finding M2,
  Decision §14.6) — qualifying commitments, completed eligible interviews, the resulting rate (and
  whether that rate meets the §14.1 item 3 50% condition), registered-surveyor qualifying commitments
  and the number of institutional respondents whose status was authoritatively checked,
  recruitment-source breakdown, and recruitment-wave breakdown. A figure such as "6 qualifying
  commitments" reported without its denominator is non-compliant with this playbook. "6 of 12" and
  "6 of 100" shall never be labelled identically;
- the deduplicated qualifying-commitment count per segment (§10 above; Decision §9), with its
  commitment-tier composition (§9 of this playbook) disclosed, never presented as a single
  undifferentiated figure;
- the referral-tagged subgroup breakdown (§8 above, main Decision §10) and the referral-concentration
  ceiling result (§8 above, Decision §14.1 item 7);
- any record excluded under Decision §14.8 / §22 below (ordered-sequence violations), the deviation
  that produced each exclusion, and the recalculated figures that follow from excluding it;
- the teach-back comprehension pass rate (§6);
- the recorded prior/current price data (§5);
- an explicit statement of which of the five outcomes at main Decision §5.3 the institutional-segment
  evidence supports, independent of the retail-segment result, together with the retail-segment
  result reported separately and never substituted for the institutional result (main Decision §9);
- any channel flagged under §15 option 2, and the resulting uncertainty disclosure;
- any interviewer-influence finding under §19 below, and any resulting INDETERMINATE classification;
- the discovery-artifact limitation statement (main Decision §16) reproduced in full;
- an explicit statement that the sample is exploratory and not statistically significant or
  population-representative (Decision §14.7).

This playbook does not itself authorize the drafting or issuance of that final report as a production
or public-facing document; report production, like every other activity described here, remains
subject to main Decision §22's exhaustive activity prohibition and to every operational prerequisite at
§16 above — GD-015's ratification satisfies §16 item 1 only, and does not by itself lift any other §16
gate.

## 18. Ordered research sequence

*Added on remediation of finding C2, making explicit the sequence this playbook's individual sections
previously left inferable.* Subject in full to the Nigerian data-protection review gate (§16 item 6)
and every other precondition in §16, the sequence for each respondent is:

1. privacy notice and informed-participation step (§21 below; Decision §25) — no substantive research
   data is collected before this step;
2. segment and recruitment-source classification (§3 above), including the registered-surveyor
   eligibility check where claimed (§3 above; Decision §7.1);
3. non-adjudication explanation (Decision §3);
4. teach-back (§6 above);
5. where teach-back fails after the permitted clarification, the interview is terminated for research
   purposes or the response is classified as a comprehension failure (§6 above) — substantive
   commitment questions are not asked of a respondent who has failed teach-back;
6. current-or-previous price-paid/price-quoted question (§5 above), asked before the proposed price
   point to avoid anchoring;
7. assignment of exactly one price point (§4 above);
8. stated-price response recorded (tier 1, §9 above);
9. the permitted costly non-financial commitment request, where applicable (tier 2, §9 above; no
   payment-backed step exists under this playbook — §20 below);
10. closing explanation, including withdrawal and contact information consistent with §21's
    informed-participation disclosures;
11. secure recording, deduplication (§7 above), and classification.

No step in this sequence authorizes collecting any document, personal data, or commitment beyond what
the main Decision and this playbook expressly permit.

## 19. Interviewer independence and script discipline

*Added on remediation of finding C6, restating Decision §26 with this playbook's own operational
detail.* Every interview or intake activity under this playbook is conducted under:

- a standardized, versioned interview script, fixed before recruitment begins;
- consistent core questions within each segment (§3 above);
- recorded interviewer identity for every interview;
- disclosure of any material relationship between an interviewer and a respondent;
- no outcome-linked reward for any interviewer or recruiter tied to conversion, commitment, or
  threshold results;
- no coaching of a respondent toward a desired answer;
- no suppression of a negative, incomplete, or withdrawn response from the recorded dataset;
- a recorded log of any deviation from the approved script;
- separate reporting of responses gathered by a programme insider or an influential intermediary
  (§8 above); and
- an INDETERMINATE classification (§12 above; Decision §5.3, §14.5) wherever interviewer influence is
  found to have materially compromised the evidence gathered.

## 20. Payment-backed commitment unavailable under this playbook

*Added on remediation of finding A3.* The three-tier commitment terminology at §9 above is retained
for conceptual clarity, but the following is unambiguous and controls over any other reading of §9:

- this playbook, and GD-015 generally, does not authorize payment, deposits, refundable reservations,
  or any other financial commitment of any kind (Decision §5.2, §22; §13 above);
- the demand test this playbook describes may measure only **tier 1 (stated-price acceptance)** and
  **tier 2 (costly non-financial commitment)**;
- **tier 3 ("payment-backed commitment") is unavailable and unmeasurable under this playbook** — no
  step in the ordered research sequence at §18 above collects it, and none may be added to collect it
  without separate financial, refund, consumer-protection, privacy, and governance authority (§13
  above);
- no result may be reported, described, or classified as payment-backed demand under this playbook;
- no §14 threshold band depends, or may be made to depend, on a tier-3 result, since none can exist
  under this authorization.

## 21. Informed participation and privacy notice

*Added on remediation of finding M6, restating Decision §25 with this playbook's own operational
placement (§18 step 1 above).* Before any interview or respondent data is collected, every respondent
shall receive, and shall be given a documented opportunity to respond to:

1. a clear explanation of the research purpose;
2. what information will be collected;
3. how it will be used;
4. who may access it;
5. the retention and deletion arrangements resolved under Decision §17 items 5–6 and the Nigerian
   data-protection review at §16 item 6 above;
6. that participation is voluntary;
7. that the participant may decline individual questions or withdraw, subject to any clearly explained
   practical limits;
8. a privacy notice;
9. identification of the applicable lawful basis or bases, as determined by the Decision §17 item 8
   review — not asserted by this playbook.

A documented indication that the person agreed to participate is recorded as part of intake. This
recorded agreement does not, by itself, resolve every applicable legal requirement. No land document,
parcel evidence, or identifying information beyond what the main Decision §6–§11 require may be
collected merely because a person agreed to participate.

## 22. Ordered-sequence violations — evidentiary consequence

*Added on remediation of the second preliminary governance review, restating Decision §14.8 with this
playbook's own operational detail.* Where a respondent record is collected out of the order §18 above
requires, the record is handled as follows, and no step in this playbook may be read to permit
otherwise:

1. a record collected before step 1 of the §18 sequence (the privacy notice and informed-participation
   step; §21 above) is completed does not count as a completed eligible institutional interview or as
   a qualifying institutional commitment, for either segment;
2. a substantive commitment response (tier 1 or tier 2; §9 above) collected before a successful
   teach-back (§18 steps 3–5) does not count toward any threshold in Decision §14;
3. the affected record is handled under the data-protection and incident-handling procedure the
   Decision §17 item 8 review establishes, not under a retention or deletion rule this playbook
   invents;
4. the deviation is logged (consistent with §19 above's deviation-log requirement) and disclosed in
   the final report (§17 above);
5. every threshold and denominator in Decision §14 is recalculated using only the records that remain
   valid after the exclusion;
6. the institutional result is `INDETERMINATE` where, after that recalculation, the valid sample falls
   below the Decision §14.0 minimum, the deviation is systemic or repeated rather than isolated,
   informed participation cannot be evidenced for the remaining sample, the integrity of the remaining
   data cannot otherwise be established, or the Decision §17 item 8 review requires suspension or
   invalidation of the exercise;
7. an isolated, documented deviation does not, by itself, invalidate the remaining, unaffected,
   compliant records, provided item 3 above's procedure permits their continued use and every other
   applicable threshold is independently satisfied using only those remaining records.

This section states which records may be counted; it is not authority to collect any record before
the operational prerequisites at §16 above are satisfied, and it does not excuse a collection that
violated them.

## 23. Duration, sequencing, and effort logging

*Added on Governance Authority parameter incorporation, operationalizing Decision §17 items 1–2.*

**Duration.** The 35-calendar-day ceiling, its one permissible 14-day extension, and the day-range
structure are set at Decision §17 item 1; this playbook adds no separate clock. The duration clock
starts only once every item in §16 above is satisfied — recording the start date is itself an §16
record, not a separate authorization. No recruitment wave (§2 above), interview, or analysis step may
be scheduled to fall outside the 35-day ceiling (or the approved extension) at the time it is
scheduled; a step already under way when day 35 is reached stops at that point unless an extension has
been separately approved before day 35.

**Effort logging.** Every contributor records, for every task performed under this playbook, their
name, the date, the activity category (matching Decision §17 item 2's five-row table), and the hours
spent, in a single running log. The log is reviewed against the 80-hour warning threshold and the
96-hour ceiling at the point of each entry, not only at the end of a wave or at the governance
checkpoint. Reaching 80 hours triggers the remaining-effort review Decision §17 item 2 requires before
any further hours are logged; reaching 96 hours stops further activity under this playbook until
Governance Authority approval of an increase is obtained. A reassignment of unused hours between
categories is recorded in the same log, with the control it was checked against (Decision §17 item 2's
"no substantive control weakened" condition) noted alongside it.

## 24. Expense and incentive administration

*Added on Governance Authority parameter incorporation, operationalizing Decision §17 items 3–4.*

**Expense administration.** Every expense is logged against the ₦600,000 ceiling at Decision §17 item
3 at the time it is incurred, with its receipt or written exception record attached, and against the
category it falls under (or, if it is excluded by item 3, it is not logged against this ceiling at
all, and is not incurred under this playbook's authority). The running expense total, inclusive of the
incentive register at this section's second part, is checked against ₦600,000 at each entry.
Expenditure that would exceed ₦600,000 is not incurred until Governance Authority approval under
Decision §17 item 3 is obtained.

**Incentive administration.** Every completed eligible interview may trigger exactly one ₦10,000
reimbursement, recorded in a payment register kept separately from research responses (Decision §17
item 4), identified only by the respondent's unique identifier (§21 above), never by a link to that
respondent's substantive answers. The register tracks, for each payment: date, respondent identifier,
segment (institutional or retail, kept distinguishable), and confirmation that the interview reached
"completed eligible" status (§18 above) before the payment was made. The register stops accepting new
entries once 15 reimbursements (₦150,000) have been recorded; a sixteenth reimbursement is not made
without separate Governance Authority approval of an increase to Decision §17 item 4. The interviewer
who conducts an interview may not be the person who decides that interview's eligibility classification
after seeing the respondent's substantive answers (Decision §17 item 4's anti-discretion control); the
eligibility classification is fixed by the ordered sequence at §18 above before any incentive decision
is made.

## 25. External-engagement conduct and escalation

*Added on Governance Authority parameter incorporation, operationalizing Decision §17 item 9.*

Before any recruitment under §2 above begins, the Project Lead and the named written alternate
required by Decision §17 item 9 are confirmed in the operational record this section requires. Every
respondent-facing or SURCON-facing action under this playbook (§3's registered-surveyor classification
check, §8's referral tagging, any explanation of Phase 0A given to a respondent) is performed only by
the Project Lead, the alternate, or an interviewer operating under the script and independence
controls at §19 above — never by a person acting outside those controls.

**Escalation.** Where a respondent, a Chief Surveyor, a professional-body officer, or any other party
asks for, proposes, or appears to expect anything on Decision §17 item 9's prohibited list — employment,
commissions, future work, a contract, MOU, or letter of intent, platform access, onboarding, an

## 26. Data retention and access — operational schedule

*Added on Governance Authority parameter incorporation, operationalizing Decision §17 items 5–6 and 8.*

The retention periods in the table at Decision §17 item 5 are implemented as calendar-date deletion
tasks, calculated from the governance checkpoint date (main Decision §5.3) or the interview date, as
each row specifies, and recorded on a deletion log distinct from the research data itself.

**Access, matching Decision §17 item 6's two-tiered structure exactly — this section adds no third
tier and no additional holder:** an interviewer conducting a specific interview (§19 above), including
the Project Lead or alternate when personally interviewing, handles that respondent's data only
task-bound to that one interview, and records it into the retained corpus immediately afterward; once
recorded, ongoing access to the retained, identifiable corpus belongs only to the Project Lead and the
one named authorized reviewer Decision §17 item 6 names, with the alternate holding that same
retained-corpus access only while formally substituting for the Project Lead, as recorded alongside the
deletion log below. No other contributor to this playbook's activities is given retained-corpus access
of any kind. Every deletion, once performed, is recorded on the deletion log with its date and the
record category deleted; a deletion that cannot be performed on schedule (for example, because a legal
obligation under Decision §17 item 5's incentive-register exception applies) is logged with the
reason, not silently skipped.

**Legal-review supremacy.** Where the Decision §17 item 8 Nigerian data-protection review, once it
occurs, shortens a retention period, strengthens an access control, or imposes a condition this section
does not already state, that outcome controls over this section without needing a separate edit to this
playbook — this section implements Decision §17 items 5–6 and 8 as currently determined, and is read
subject to whatever the required review later adds or tightens, consistent with Decision §17 item 5's
own "legal review may shorten... without needing that approval" rule.
